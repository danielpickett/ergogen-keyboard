# Pickett46 case fit prototypes

The 1.5 mm switch plates use the [canonical DXFs](../docs/plates/) with
clearance for the socketed nice!nano. The trays use the current routed PCB
perimeters, with a 0.45 mm perimeter gap and 6 mm of space below the PCB.

| Part | Left | Right | Editable source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Switch plate | [STL](pickett46-left-plate-controller-clearance.stl) | [STL](pickett46-right-plate-controller-clearance.stl) | [OpenSCAD](pickett46-plate.scad) |
| Tray | [STL](pickett46-left-fit.stl) | [STL](pickett46-right-fit.stl) | [OpenSCAD](pickett46-tray.scad) |

The plate-to-tray attachment, relocated battery jack and mated plug clearance,
and assembled USB and switch access still need a physical fit check.
